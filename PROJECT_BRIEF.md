## Website goals
1. Establish a premium and trustworthy visual brand.
2. Showcase services and event categories.
3. Communicate that packages start at $300.
4. Encourage customers to enquire for a customised package.
5. Make phone contact easy.
6. Show a polished booking/enquiry UI prototype.

## Content guardrails
Do not invent:
- Exact package inclusions
- Detailed prices beyond the $300 starting price
- Company history or years of experience
- Street address, email, or social links
- Customer names, reviews, client logos, awards, or project metrics
- Claims that stock photos depict the company's own work

Use editable placeholders for missing information.
